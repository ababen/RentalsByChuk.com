# Rental-season calendar (WPB · Palm Beach Gardens · Jupiter)

The signal agent adds +15 to signals that match the current window. These are the site's own claims
(STRATEGY.md, compare.ts); edit as the market teaches you.

| months | window | renter angle | landlord angle |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jan–Feb | Peak season | Seasonal stock is thin; book next season now. WEF season in Wellington. | Lock next winter's seasonal tenant early. |
| Mar–Apr | Season ends | Snowbirds leaving; annual leases from May. | Seasonal vs. annual decision for next year. |
| May–Jun | Goldilocks window | Seasonal units turn annual; best selection and negotiating room before summer. | Price right now or sit through summer. |
| Jul–Aug | School-year moves + early snowbird booking | Relocating for work: start 6–8 weeks before school. Snowbirds: "text me in the summer." | List seasonal homes for winter now. |
| Sep–Oct | Pre-season | Seasonal shoppers pick from what's left; HOA approvals take weeks. | Accidental landlords: homes sitting unsold convert to rentals. |
| Nov–Dec | Season starts | Approvals and move-ins; last seasonal options. | Get the HOA approval packet in before the holidays. |

Evergreen any month: HOA rules by community, neighborhood spotlights, rent vs. sell, monthly market numbers.
