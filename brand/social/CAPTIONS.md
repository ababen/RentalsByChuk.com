# RentalsByChuk social kit

Graphics are in `posts/`:
- `01`–`06` are feed posts, 1080×1350 (4:5). They work on Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn.
- `07`–`08` are stories and reels covers, 1080×1920.

The video is `video/contact-chuk.mp4`. Rebuild everything with `node tools/make-social.js` and `node tools/make-contact-video.js` (see the header of each file).

## Ground rules (the LoKation compliance pass of Oct 26)

- **No fee or pay claims.** Don't write "$0 for renters", "no cost", "free", "the landlord pays", or any fee amount.
- **Fair housing.** Don't describe who a place suits ("family-friendly", "great for kids"). Describe the home and the service.
- **Every post names the agent and brokerage.** Use "Alexander "Chuk" Babenchuk · LoKation Real Estate" and the phone number (561) 220-9399. The graphics already carry all three.
- **Only claim what the site says:**
  - rentals only, in West Palm Beach, Palm Beach Gardens and Jupiter;
  - he finds the home, checks the HOA first, negotiates the lease and chases the approval;
  - movers and move-in are coordinated, but the work is done by independent local companies;
  - remote relocations;
  - the 48-hour rental analysis for landlords;
  - the goal of keys in about 21 days.
- **No made-up testimonials.** If you want them, ask real clients and post their exact words with their permission.
