# RentalsByChuk social kit

Graphics are in `posts/`:
- `01`–`06` are feed posts, 1080×1350 (4:5). They work on Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn.
- `07`–`08` are stories and reels covers, 1080×1920.

Rebuild them with `node tools/make-social.js` (see the header of that file).

Every claim below comes from RentalsByChuk.com: rentals only, in three cities; $0 for renters; free HOA rental rules; the 48-hour rental analysis; keys in about 21 days. Keep it that way, and don't make up client quotes or results. If you want testimonials, ask real clients and post their exact words with their permission.

**Always include:** RentalsByChuk.com, *LoKation Real Estate*, and the phone number (561) 220-9399 (the graphics already carry all three).
