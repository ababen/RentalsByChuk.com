RENTALS BY CHUK

Brand Style Guide

How RentalsByChuk looks, sounds and stays compliant across business cards, flyers, brochures, signs and the web. Version 1 · October 2026.

TAGLINE
Your next rental, and move, handled.

Set “handled.” in Cormorant italic, coral on navy (deep coral on light grounds). Break the line after the comma when space is tight. Don’t paraphrase it.

01 · Logo

The “R” monogram is a house key crossed with a palm frond: the stem is the key, the aqua and coral strokes are the fronds. It always sits in a rounded tile (corner radius = 25% of the tile). The wordmark is Sora ExtraBold in caps with “BY” in coral.

Do
Keep clear space equal to the tile’s height on all sides. Minimum size: tile 0.25 in (24 px) wide; lockup 1.25 in wide. Use the monogram alone for favicons, social avatars and sign riders.
Don’t
Stretch, outline, recolor or add shadows. Never set the logo on photos without a solid tile behind it, and never on coral or aqua grounds, which make the fronds vibrate.

02 · Color

03 · Typography

Three families, each with one job. All are open-source Google Fonts, so print shops and Canva can match them.

ROLE SPECIMEN SETTINGS
Display Cormorant Garamond Bold 700, italic 600 for emphasis. Headlines, prices, phone numbers. Tight leading (0.9–1.05), tracking −2%.
Label SORA · KICKERS 700–800, all caps, tracking +14–16%. Kickers, city lines, the wordmark. Never for sentences.
Body Inter reads cleanly at any size. 400 / 600 / 700. Print body 11–12 pt at 1.5 line height; web 16–17 px. Fine print no smaller than 7 pt on cards, 9 pt elsewhere.

04 · Voice

Sound like a neighbor who happens to be an expert: short sentences, specific places, no jargon.

Direct
“We price by neighborhood, not zip code.”
Helpful
“Here’s the math: seasonal vs. annual, side by side.”
Calm and capable
“HOA rules checked before you apply.”
Local
Name the board, the street, the community. Never “Palm Beach County area.”

Cities always run West Palm Beach • Palm Beach Gardens • Jupiter, in that order. Calls to action lead with texting: “Text Chuk.”

05 · The Avatar

The 3D Chuk avatar is the face of the brand: warm, a little playful, always helpful. Blue eyes, salt-and-pepper beard, white blazer over a black tee. He appears on every piece, one pose per piece.

Wave
Covers, website hero, first hellos.
Thumbs-up
Calls to action, flyers, closing slides.
Head
Contact blocks, cards, avatars, favicons.
EXPRESSIONS

Smile (default) · talking · laughing · thoughtful · surprised · wink. Match the expression to the message: thoughtful for HOA rules, laughing for move-in day.

TURNAROUND

Front, three-quarter, side and back. The reference for video, animation and new poses: keep proportions, eye color and wardrobe consistent.

Do
Crop in an arch or a circle (white ring, navy keyline) on sand or paper. Keep him upright, facing into the page, with clear space around his head.
Don’t
Recolor, stretch, flip text-facing, add props or speech that implies guarantees, or place him over busy photos. Never mix two poses on one piece.

Master files live in brand/chuk/ in the website repo.

06 · Compliance

Every printed or digital ad must carry:

  • Agent name as licensed: Alexander “Chuk” Babenchuk, REALTOR®
  • Brokerage: LoKation Real Estate, at least as prominent as required by the broker
  • License: FL Lic. SL3596634
  • The Equal Housing Opportunity logo or statement

Avoid in all copy:

  • “Free,” “no cost,” or any statement about who pays fees or commissions
  • Fair-housing red flags that describe people instead of homes (“family-friendly,” “perfect for kids,” “ideal for singles”)
  • Guarantees or invented stats; “keys in about 21 days” is always framed as a goal
