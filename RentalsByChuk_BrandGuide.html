01 — Logo System

The Mark

The "R" monogram is a key + palm hybrid. The stem is a house-key; the bowl is a palm frond. Works at 16px favicon to 6ft yard sign. Wordmark is custom-tuned Sora + Cormorant for Chuk's signature.

Primary — Navy on Sand (web header, business cards)
Reversed — White on Navy (footer, yard sign, merch)

Wordmark Only

RENTALSBYCHUK
For tight bars, email signatures, favicons. Never stack without the R.

Monogram Only

R

R
App icon, social avatar, map pins, watermarks.

Lockup Rule

Minimum clear space = height of the R on all sides.
Minimum size: 24px tall (digital), 1" (print).
Never rotate, stretch, or add drop shadow.
Coral BY is fixed — don't recolor it.

Don't

  • Use coral as background for navy logo (vibrates)
  • Put the R in a circle — it's a squircle (14px radius)
  • Swap tagline cities — always WPB • PBG • Jupiter in that order

Do

  • Pair with HomesByChuk navy on same page (sister lockup)
  • Use Aqua underline (3px) under tagline — signature
  • Let the R be the favicon / Apple touch icon

04 — Voice & Photography

Sound Like a Neighbor Who Happens to Be an Expert

Voice Principles

  • Direct: "We price by neighborhood, not zip code."
  • Helpful: "Here's the math — seasonal vs. annual, side by side."
  • A little cocky: "Most agents treat rentals as a side hustle. We made it the whole business."
  • Local: Name the board, the street, the school. Never "Palm Beach County area."

Photography

  • Golden-hour, vertical video first (Reels/TikTok native)
  • No fake HDR — warm, lived-in, palm shadows allowed
  • People > empty rooms: show the village (Abacoa green, Clematis at night)
  • Overlay: subtle sand gradient, never heavy filters
